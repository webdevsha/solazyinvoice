Strategic Coaching & Transition Agreement

This Strategic Coaching & Transition Agreement (the "Agreement") is made effective as of [Effective Date], by and between:

Client: [Your Name] ("the Client"), operating as a solopreneur currently in a transition phase validating multiple professional paths (including grant capture, part-time consulting, and product execution).

Strategic Coach: R ("the Coach"), providing strategic alignment, focused troubleshooting, and validation coaching.

WHEREAS, the Client is navigating a strategic transition to identify, validate, and optimize revenue streams and focus domains, specifically targeting pathways including Cetalabs, Kinder, Grant Applications, and Consulting Contracts; and

WHEREAS, the Client seeks a professional coach, strategic foil, and structured accountability partner to pressure-test decisions, validate models, and review tactical approaches without the typical isolation of a solopreneur;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties mutually agree to enter this professional coaching framework:

1. Scope of Strategic Coaching Services

The Coach is retained to provide systematic path-validation, focus-filtering, and strategic pressure-testing. The scope of coaching comprises:

  • Path Validation Coaching: Serving as an objective sounding board to evaluate tradeoffs between pursuing grants, accepting part-time consulting contracts, or investing in entrepreneurship.
  • Grant Proposal Narrative Review: Co-strategizing alignment, identifying structural risk factors, and clarifying impact storytelling for competitive funding applications.
  • Proposal & Negotiation Advisory: Helping structure, price, and position client-facing retainers to protect the Client's cognitive space and financial margins.
  • Accountability & Focus Maintenance: Regular alignment sessions to dismantle overwhelm, establish clear milestone targets, and manage transition timelines.

2. Hybrid Coaching Fee & Performance Premium

To establish a clear, high-dignity professional transaction while protecting the early-phase cash flow of a transitioning solopreneur, the fee structure is balanced as follows:

A. Hourly Coaching Base: The Coach shall bill at a rate of RM 100 per hour for structured coaching, strategy reviews, and alignment calls. Base hours are capped at 10 hours per month unless explicitly agreed otherwise in writing. If the Client requires additional hours beyond the monthly cap, the Coach will notify the Client before proceeding, and additional hours will be billed at the same RM 100/hour rate.

B. Success-Linked Performance Premium: To align focus and reward measurable impact, the Coach shall receive a 10% success premium on the following success milestones, subject to the attribution criteria outlined below:

Eligible Success Milestones:

  • Grant funding or non-dilutive capital secured during this engagement, where the Coach provided direct input on the proposal (narrative review, alignment strategy, or submission planning).
  • Consulting contracts or retainers closed during this engagement, where the Coach provided direct input on pricing, positioning, or negotiation strategy.
  • First-year launch revenue from validated startup platforms (Cetalabs, Kinder, or other portfolio ventures) launched or relaunched during this engagement, where the Coach provided direct input on GTM strategy, positioning, or business model design. This direct launch revenue share is strictly capped at 10% of the first RM 50,000 in revenue per platform (representing a maximum of RM 5,000 per platform).

Attribution Criteria:

  • The success premium applies only to milestones where the Coach provided documented strategic input (via coaching sessions, written feedback, or recorded strategy calls).
  • If the Client independently secures a milestone without the Coach's direct involvement, no premium applies.
  • In cases of ambiguous attribution, the Client and Coach will mutually agree on a fair premium amount (0-10%) based on the level of involvement.
  • The success premium is one-time per milestone (not recurring). For example, a RM 50,000 grant yields a one-time RM 5,000 premium.

Premium Cap: To protect against disproportionate upside, the total success premium in any 12-month period is capped at RM 25,000, unless mutually agreed otherwise in writing.

3. Solopreneur Safeguards & Collaborative Trust

Successful coaching depends on mutual trust, ultimate transparency, and absolute safety:

  • Dignity of Exchange: This is a professional collaboration founded on real strategic capability, not a patronizing assistance model. The Coach's feedback delivers objective value, and compensation is paid for professional services.
  • The Feedback Guardrail: Coaching requires radical, unfiltered truth. Tough critiques regarding grant narratives, business models, or pricing structures must be delivered constructively and received as professional course-corrections.
  • Capacity & Blueprint Building: This framework serves as a model. The Coach is actively encouraged to package this exact dynamic (Strategic Path-Validation Coaching for Transitioning Founders) as a template to capture more high-value consulting clients.

4. Invoicing Protocols & Payment Terms

The Coach shall submit monthly invoices for deposit and completed coaching hours. Success premiums shall be invoiced separately upon confirmation of milestone achievement (e.g., grant approval notification, contract signing, first revenue receipt).

💡 Ihsan Invoicing Terms: Standard monthly base invoices are payable within three (3) calendar days of receipt to honor swift labor compensation. Success premium invoices are payable within thirty (30) calendar days of milestone confirmation.

5. Term and Termination

This Agreement operates on a rolling, monthly schedule. To minimize pressure and accommodate the fast-changing conditions of career pivots, either party may terminate this agreement instantly upon written notification. All logged coaching hours completed prior to termination shall be fully settled.

6. Jurisdiction

This framework shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the laws of Selangor, Malaysia.

❤️ 7. Romantic Relationship Priority & Boundary Protocols

This professional coaching engagement exists within the context of an active romantic relationship between the Client and the Coach. To protect the health, intimacy, and longevity of the personal relationship, the following safeguards are hereby established:

A. Relationship-First Principle

The romantic relationship takes precedence over this professional engagement. If at any point the professional dynamic threatens the health of the personal relationship, either party may immediately:

  • Pause the engagement for a defined period (1 week to 1 month) to allow the relationship to stabilize.
  • Terminate the engagement without penalty, financial obligation beyond already-completed work, or relational consequence.
  • Renegotiate terms (scope, hours, communication style) to restore relational health.

Neither party shall hold resentment, guilt, or leverage related to pausing or terminating this professional arrangement. The relationship is more important than the contract.

B. Work-Free Zones (Time Boundaries)

To prevent professional obligations from consuming personal time and intimacy, the following work-free zones are established:

  • No work discussions after 8:00 PM on weekdays unless mutually agreed in advance for urgent deadlines.
  • Fridays after Maghrib through Saturday evening are designated relationship-only time — no coaching sessions, no invoice discussions, no grant reviews.
  • During meals together, date nights, or designated quality time, work topics are off-limits unless the Client explicitly requests strategic input in the moment.

Violation of work-free zones: If either party repeatedly brings work into protected time, the other party may invoke a 24-hour work silence — no professional communication for one full day to reset boundaries.

C. Feedback Firewall (Emotional Safety)

Professional feedback (critiques of grant proposals, strategic misalignments, missed deliverables) must be compartmentalized from personal worth or romantic evaluation.

  • Professional critiques are not personal attacks: If the Client provides tough feedback on the Coach's strategic input, the Coach agrees not to internalize this as rejection or inadequacy in the relationship.
  • Romantic reassurance after tough feedback: If a coaching session involves hard critiques, the session ends with relational affirmation — a simple acknowledgment: "That was tough feedback, but it doesn't change how I feel about you."
  • Separate "work frustration" from "relationship frustration": If the Client is frustrated with a missed deadline or vague deliverable, that frustration stays in the professional lane. It does not spill into relational conflict.
  • Cooling-off protocol: If professional feedback triggers defensiveness, hurt, or conflict, either party may call a 2-hour pause before continuing the conversation to allow emotional regulation.

D. Power Dynamic Neutralization

To prevent the Client from becoming "the boss" and the Coach from becoming "the subordinate" in the romantic relationship:

  • Invoicing and payment are transactional, not relational. The Coach submits invoices professionally (no guilt, no hedging). The Client pays on time (no power plays, no withholding). Neither party uses payment as leverage in personal conflicts.
  • The Coach retains full professional autonomy. The Client may provide feedback and strategic input, but the Coach is not obligated to follow directives. This is a coaching partnership, not an employer-employee relationship.
  • Reciprocal vulnerability: The Client actively seeks areas where the Coach can coach, advise, or support her (even outside this formal scope) to maintain relational balance (e.g. personal goals or relationship dynamics).
  • No "boss tone" in personal contexts. If the Client finds herself using a directive or managerial tone outside of scheduled work sessions, she commits to pausing and resetting to a relational tone.

E. Success Premium Gratitude Ritual (Joy Reclamation)

When a success premium is triggered (grant secured, contract closed, revenue milestone hit), the payout is celebrated together as a shared win, not just a transactional payment.

Required ritual: Within 7 days of a success milestone, the Client and Coach will:

  • Share a celebratory meal or date to honor the collaborative effort.
  • Acknowledge each other's contributions (not just the Coach's strategic input, but also the Client's execution, resilience, and vision).
  • Redirect a portion of the success premium (at the Coach's discretion) toward a shared relationship investment — a weekend trip, a special experience, or a meaningful gift for both.

F. Conflict Escalation Safeguard

If a professional disagreement escalates into a personal conflict (yelling, stonewalling, blame, contempt):

  • Immediate 24-hour pause on all professional and relationship discussions. Both parties take space.
  • Third-party mediation option: Either party may request a neutral third-party conversation facilitator (therapist, trusted mutual friend, or relationship coach) to help navigate the conflict before resuming professional work.
  • Auto-termination trigger: If three (3) professional conflicts escalate into damaging personal conflicts within a 6-month period, the professional engagement auto-terminates to protect the relationship. All completed work is settled with no relational penalty.

G. Regular Relationship Check-Ins (Separate from Work)

To ensure the professional engagement isn't eroding intimacy, the Client and Coach commit to:

  • Monthly relationship health check-ins (15-30 minutes) exploring:
    • Are we spending too much time in "work mode" and not enough in "us mode"?
    • Is one of us feeling more like a boss/employee than a partner?
    • Are we celebrating wins together, or is the work creating distance?
    • Do we need to adjust boundaries, hours, or communication style?
  • Quarterly "pause and assess" — Every 3 months, both parties explicitly ask: "Should we continue this professional engagement, or is it time to wind down to protect the relationship?"

H. Exit Strategy with Relational Grace

  • No residual resentment: Both parties agree that ending the professional work does not mean the relationship failed. It means prioritizing the romantic bond over the business arrangement.
  • Gratitude over grievance: The final professional conversation includes mutual acknowledgment of what worked, what was learned, and appreciation for the effort.
  • Clean financial closure: All outstanding invoices are settled within 30 days to protect relational trust.
  • Re-engagement option: If the relationship stabilizes and both parties want to try the professional engagement again later, they may do so only after a minimum 3-month gap to ensure the relationship has fully reset.
